# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- New: battleground tracker. At the end of every battleground (as soon as the winner is known) all players of the scoreboard are stored in a separate account-wide database (`ForeverPlayerStatisticsBG`). Each entry has name, faction, class, race, level, killing blows, honorable kills, deaths, honor, damage and healing, plus map, winner, your faction and level, and duration. The scoreboard has no levels. They are collected during the match from the raid, enemy nameplates, target and mouseover, otherwise taken from the player database.
- New: "Battleground Statistics" window (button "Battlegrounds" in the overview or `/fps bg`). It shows wins per faction, your own wins and losses, and the win rate per class, race and level. You can filter by map, bracket (from your level) and team. Click a class, race or level to filter the other lists. Every player of a finished battleground counts as one game, a win if their faction won. Draws are not counted. Recording can be switched off in the window or with `/fps bg off`.
- The battleground window has a "Battlegrounds" list: one row "All battlegrounds" and one row per battleground. Each row shows the number of games, how often Alliance and Horde won, your own record, and the win rate of the current filter (team, class, race or level). Click a row to show only that battleground in the whole window (class, race and level win rates included). Click it again, or click "All battlegrounds", to see all of them.
- New: second minimap button (PvP banner) for the battleground statistics. Its tooltip shows the number of recorded battlegrounds, your wins and losses over all battlegrounds and per battleground, and your last battleground. Left-click opens the battleground window, right-click the player overview. It can be dragged around the minimap on its own. The icon turns grey while recording is off. Hide or show it with `/fps bgicon`.
- The battleground minimap tooltip says that it is part of ForeverPlayerStatistics, and both minimap tooltips show whether ForeverPlayerStatistics and battleground recording are enabled or disabled (the main one also shows the `/who` search). How to disable is shown in red, how to enable in green.
- Battleground recording and the battleground minimap button can also be switched in Esc -> Options -> AddOns. Both settings are saved permanently.
- Every option checkbox shows a tooltip with its current state (enabled / disabled), "Click to disable" in red or "Click to enable" in green, and whether the setting is saved permanently.

- Fixed: "Only this realm" showed only about 50 players. On some logins the game returns an empty realm name. The addon then stored new players without a realm, and the filter only matched the players found since that login. Players saved without a realm were also dropped at the next `/reload` or login. The addon now remembers the last realm name and uses it when the game returns none. Players already saved without a realm are moved to that realm at the next login.

- New: hover over a player name in chat to see what the database knows about that player: level, race, class, faction, guild, zone, and when the player was last and first seen. Names that are not in the database show "Not in the ForeverPlayerStatistics database yet". You can switch this off in the overview window, in Esc -> Options -> AddOns, or with `/fps chat`.
- New: hovering a player name in chat also refreshes that player. The game only allows `/who` on a key press or click, so the addon queues `/who n-"First Last"` and sends it with your next key press or click, before the next scan query. It also works while the scan is stopped. The tooltip shows the state ("Refresh with your next key press or click", "Refreshing...", "Refreshed just now", "Not online") and updates when the answer arrives. The same player is refreshed at most once a minute. Players of the other faction cannot be refreshed, because `/who` only lists your own faction. Switch it off in Esc -> Options -> AddOns or with `/fps chatwho`.
- New: the chat tooltip shows how old the level is ("Level from 3 h ago"). It is green under one hour and white under one day. After one day it turns orange with a warning: "(!) Level from 2 days ago - may be outdated".
- New: average and median level in the overview, below the players-per-level chart. They follow the faction, realm, class and race filters, so clicking a class shows its average and median level.
- New: "Show: count / level" button in the overview. "level" switches the class and race lists to the average and median level of each class or race, sorted by average level ("Sort: name" still works). The tooltip of each row shows the number of players and the average and median level in both views.
- New: level range bar below the players-per-level chart. Drag its handles to count only players from level X to Y: the Alliance vs Horde bar, the class and race lists with their percentages, and the average and median level then use only that range. Levels outside the range are dimmed in the chart. Right-click the bar for all levels again. The range is saved.

## 1.0.1

- Fixed: druids were not recorded and were missing from the statistics. The game numbers the druid class 11 but reports fewer classes in classic clients, so the druid was treated as an unknown class.
- Fixed: "script ran too long" error with large databases when hovering over the minimap button or while the overview was open. The statistics no longer go through every stored player each time. The addon keeps counts per realm, faction, class, race and level up to date as players are stored, so the overview and tooltip stay fast with any number of players.
- Fixed: error "attempted to index a table that cannot be indexed with secret keys" when targeting or mousing over players in battlegrounds (and other places where the game hides unit data from addons). Such players are now skipped instead.
- Fixed: large databases could no longer be loaded. From roughly 50,000 players on, WoW refused the saved file with "Error loading ... ForeverPlayerStatistics.lua: constant table overflow", and the addon started with an empty database. Players are now saved packed into a few text lines, so the database loads with any realistic number of players (hundreds of millions) and the file is about a third of its old size.
- The new format is written automatically at the next logout or `/reload`. Databases that are still small enough to load are converted without any action.
- A database that already hit the error cannot be loaded by the game. Keep the file (with WoW closed, make a copy of `WTF/Account/<account>/SavedVariables/ForeverPlayerStatistics.lua`) before you log in again, because the next logout replaces it with the empty database. It can then be converted outside the game.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release, for WoW Forever only.
- Continuous `/who` scan that starts automatically and runs until you press Stop, also over /reload and logout. A query is sent with your next key press or click in the game world every few seconds (the game only allows `/who` on a key press or click). Each pass asks every level once without any other filter, from the highest level down to 1 (`60-60`, `59-59`, ... `1-1`), then starts again. Optional deep search (overview checkbox or `/fps deep`) adds a random name letter to each level query (`23-23 n-"k"`) to find other players on busy levels. Scan queries run in the background: no Who window and no chat output, also when the server answers a query late (after the 10 s timeout).
- Options (overview window, Esc -> Options -> AddOns, slash commands) to switch the whole addon off or the `/who` search off permanently, or to pause the `/who` search until logout.
- Records players you target or mouse over (including the other faction) and the results of your own `/who` searches.
- Supports the Skyborne race on both factions (e.g. "Himmelsgeborener der Windformer" in German /who results). The race list shows them separately as "Skyborne (A)" and "Skyborne (H)".
- Players whose class, race, faction or level is unknown are not stored and are left out of all statistics.
- All stored values are English, whatever the client language (zones via a built-in German -> English list). Zones that are not in the list are stored as the server sends them. The realm field holds the WoW Forever super server name exactly as the game shows it.
- WoW Forever names are always stored as first and last name (for targeted players the last name was taken for the realm).
- Account-wide database (`ForeverPlayerStatisticsDB`). Each player is stored once per name and realm; seeing a player again replaces the old data.
- Overview window from the minimap spyglass or `/fps`: Alliance vs Horde, classes, races and players per level, filterable by faction and realm. Click a level bar to see the classes and races of that level, click a class or race to see its players per level and the other list for it; the selections can be combined ("Show all" clears them). Class and race lists can be sorted by count or by name.
